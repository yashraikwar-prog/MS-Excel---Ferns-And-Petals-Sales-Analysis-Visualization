# 🌸 Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis

### 📁 Project Overview

This project presents a detailed sales analysis for **Ferns and Petals (FNP)**—a gifting company well-known for delivering gifts for festivals and special occasions such as Diwali, Raksha Bandhan, Holi, Valentine’s Day, Birthdays, and Anniversaries.
Using the provided dataset containing order details, customer information, products, and delivery timings, the goal is to uncover meaningful insights that help optimize sales strategy and improve customer satisfaction.


## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall business performance using key KPIs.
* Identify trends in customer spending, product popularity, and city-wise demand.
* Explore delivery efficiency through order vs. delivery time metrics.
* Compare occasion-wise revenue and identify high-performing events.
* Build an interactive and insightful dashboard for stakeholders.


## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis

The dashboard focuses on answering critical business questions, including:

### ✔ **1. Total Revenue**

Calculation of total revenue generated across all orders.

### ✔ **2. Average Order & Delivery Time**

Insights into operational performance and delivery efficiency.

### ✔ **3. Monthly Sales Performance (2023)**

Understanding monthly sales trends and seasonal spikes.

### ✔ **4. Top Products by Revenue**

Identifying the highest revenue-generating products.

### ✔ **5. Customer Spending Analysis**

Analyzing average and total spending patterns.

### ✔ **6. Sales by Top 5 Products**

Tracking the performance of the most popular products.

### ✔ **7. Top 10 Cities by Number of Orders**

Identifying regions with the highest customer activity.

### ✔ **8. Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time**

Exploring whether larger orders cause delivery delays.

### ✔ **9. Revenue Comparison Between Occasions**

Analyzing which festivals contribute the most revenue.

### ✔ **10. Product Popularity by Occasion**

Mapping which products are most preferred during specific events.
